---
title: Team Admin Guide
description: Instructions for team administrators on managing teams, members, and sprints.
---

As a Team Admin, you have additional responsibilities to ensure your team is set up correctly for morale tracking.

## 1. Creating and Managing Teams

If you have the necessary permissions, you can create a new team. As the creator, you automatically become the **Team Admin**.

*   **Team Name**: Choose a clear name for your team.
*   **Settings**: You can update the team name or delete the team if it's no longer needed.

## 2. Managing Team Members

To track morale, you need to invite your teammates:
1.  Go to the **Team Management** section.
2.  Generate an **Invitation Link**.
3.  Share this link with your colleagues. Once they accept, they will appear in the member list.
4.  You can remove members from the team if they leave the project.

## 3. Managing Sprints

Morale tracking is organized around **Sprints**.
*   **Create Sprints**: You must define start and end dates for your sprints (usually 2 weeks).
*   **Active Sprint**: Only one sprint can be active at a time for mood recording.
*   **History**: You can view past sprints to analyze morale trends over time.

## 4. Transferring Admin Rights

If you are leaving the team or want someone else to manage it, you can transfer your Team Admin rights to another member of the team from the team settings.
