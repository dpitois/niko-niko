Welcome to Niko Niko Calendar! This guide will help you get started with the application.

## 1. Getting Started

To use the application, you must first log in using one of the supported OAuth providers:
*   GitHub
*   Google
*   Discord

Once logged in, you will be redirected to your personal dashboard.

## 2. Your Dashboard

The dashboard is your central hub where you can:
*   View the teams you are a member of.
*   See the current active sprints for your teams.
*   Quickly access the mood entry page.

## 3. Recording Your Mood

The core feature of Niko Niko Calendar is tracking your daily morale.
1.  Navigate to the **Mood Entry** page.
2.  Select the date for which you want to record your mood (must be within the current sprint and not in the future).
3.  Choose the emoji that best represents your feeling:
    *   🤩 Great
    *   😊 Good
    *   😐 Neutral
    *   ☹️ Bad
    *   😫 Terrible
4.  Submit your entry. Your mood will be shared with your team anonymously (collective view).

## 4. Viewing Team Morale

You can view the collective mood of your team by navigating to the team's calendar view. This helps in understanding the overall team sentiment and identifying trends.

## 5. Badges and Gamification

By consistently recording your mood, you can earn badges! Check your profile to see your achievements and encourage your teammates to participate.
